Introduction

A Greener & Cleaner
Naroda for All

We aim to create a sustainable environment by planting 28,855 trees, promoting cleanliness, and encouraging community participation across every ward of Naroda.

Our Vision

A future where Naroda is a model of urban greenery and civic cleanliness for all of India.

Our Mission

To plant 28,855 trees and conduct 80 cleanliness drives to mark India's 80th Independence Year.

Our Goal

Unite every citizen, school, organization, and society to become stewards of their environment.

Visionary Leadership

Inspirations

Our campaign is inspired by the vision of India's leaders for a greener, cleaner nation.

Shri Narendra Modi ji

Prime Minister of India

Message
"Through national initiatives such as Swachh Bharat Mission, Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam, and Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment), the Hon'ble Prime Minister has encouraged environmental responsibility and citizen participation as important elements of nation-building. These public initiatives inspire Mission 28,855 to strengthen environmental awareness and community participation at the local level."

Shri Amit Shah ji

Home Minister of India

Message
"Public initiatives such as Hariyali Gandhinagar Lok Sabha have highlighted the importance of community participation in expanding green cover and promoting environmental stewardship. Mission 28,855 is inspired by this spirit of local participation and collective responsibility, seeking to build a cleaner and greener Naroda through active involvement of citizens and institutions."
